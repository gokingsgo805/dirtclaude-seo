Frequently Asked Questions

What is dirtClaude.ai?

An AI mineral prospecting map that combines live BLM claims, historical USGS mine records, LiDAR, and a Claude-powered geologist so you can see where the ground is still holding out.

How does the AI prediction score work?

A Weights of Evidence + Random Forest hybrid trained on each state's own mine records, districts, and placer workings. Scores any point 1–99 and shows a heat surface plus known quartz veins.

Is it free?

Yes. Rockhound plan is free forever (land ownership, water, meteorites, basic mine features, offline GPS). Pro unlocks claims, full mine records, LiDAR, predictions, and Auggie.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Download Field Packs of the layers you have on. GPS breadcrumbs, waypoints, and GPX export work with no signal.

What data sources?

USGS MRDS & 3DEP, BLM MLRS & Surface Management, state geological surveys, NRCS soils, state water/oil-gas agencies. Screening only — verify claims with BLM before staking.
